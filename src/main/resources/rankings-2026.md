The app default is `ppr`. Switch to `espn_standard` in Settings if your league is non-PPR.

### Aging Curve Rules of Thumb
- **RBs**: Significant decline risk after age 28. CMC (30), Henry (32), Barkley (29) are red flags.
- **WRs**: Peak 24-29. Jefferson (27), Chase (26), Lamb (27) still prime.
- **QBs**: Elite can play into mid-30s (Mahomes, Allen). Most decline after 33.
- **TEs**: Kelce (37) — hold only if desperate. Kittle (32), Andrews (31) — injury-prone age range.
